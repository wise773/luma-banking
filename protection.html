These are design goals for a future service—not features currently running in this static concept.

Explain unusual signals

A live service should tell you what looks different and avoid treating an automated signal as proof of fraud.

Make it safe to pause

Clear alerts should offer practical steps to verify a request before you send money or share information.

Keep a human path open

People need accessible support and a way to challenge an alert or restore access if a check goes wrong.

People stay in control
