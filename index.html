For the everyday money puzzle

See your next move, sooner.

For people balancing bills, goals and accounts in different places, Luma is designed to make the next useful step easier to spot.

Cash-flow cues that help

An opt-in view could bring recurring bills and connected balances together, making it easier to plan around what's coming up.

Scam-aware payment checks

A future service could offer a clear pause-and-check prompt when a payment seems unusual, and explain why it appeared.

One consented money view

Connect eligible banks and mobile money only with your permission, then review or disconnect those connections when you choose.

A product principle, not a live service

Safety that helps you stay in control.

This concept does not monitor accounts or detect fraud. A real protection service should explain its signals, let people pause or challenge decisions, and offer a clear path to human help.

AI and privacy, in plain language

Helpful intelligence starts with permission.

Luma is a product concept. This page does not connect to a bank, run an AI model, process payments, monitor identity, or save information you enter.

Choose what to connect

A real service should ask before using financial data, explain why it needs access, and make revoking permission straightforward.

Understand each suggestion

Guidance should show the relevant context, avoid presenting estimates as certainty, and leave the decision with you.

Get help and stay in control

A live product needs clear ways to review permissions, request data deletion, challenge an alert and reach human support.

Before launch: publish the service operator, regulated banking partners, supported regions, data providers, retention policy and verified security contacts. The names and figures in this preview are illustrative—not evidence of a live financial service.
